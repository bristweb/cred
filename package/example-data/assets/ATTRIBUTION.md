# Asset attribution

## Icons (`icons/`)

Platform logos in `icons/` (**Google**, **Yelp**, **Facebook**) are from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/) ([CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)).

All marks belong to their owners and are used only to identify where each review was posted. This example is not affiliated with those platforms.

Placeholder award badges (`award-choice.svg`, `award-bestof.svg`) are original graphics for this example (not real award marks). Any award names in `config.js` are fictional; each entry shows its label and year on the accolade card.

Placeholder company marks (`contoso-logistics.svg`, `summit-athletics.svg`) are original graphics for this example (not real brands).

Placeholder credential badges (`credential-mechanic.svg`, `credential-ebike.svg`) are original graphics for this example; the credentials and issuers in `config.js` are fictional.

## Images, video and audio (`images/`, `video/`, `audio/`)

Fictional sample media for the bundled example data, made for this repo and released with it under the MIT License: the thank-you note image (`images/`), the video poster (`images/`) and clip (`video/`), and the voicemail clip (`audio/`; synthesized speech: [Piper](https://github.com/rhasspy/piper) with the en_US ljspeech voice, trained on the public-domain [LJ Speech](https://keithito.com/LJ-Speech-Dataset/) dataset). Reviewer avatars in `images/reviewers/` are generated placeholder graphics.
