# Media

Fictional sample media for the bundled example data, made for this repo and released with it under the MIT License: the thank-you note image, the video poster and clip, and the voicemail clip (synthesized speech: [Piper](https://github.com/rhasspy/piper) with the en_US ljspeech voice, trained on the public-domain [LJ Speech](https://keithito.com/LJ-Speech-Dataset/) dataset).
